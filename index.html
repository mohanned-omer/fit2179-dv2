Act 1 · The big picture

Forty years of building a tech nation

Australia's technology workforce grew from a tiny niche to one of the country's largest professional sectors. The path was turbulent a dotcom boom and bust, a decade-long rebuild, a COVID surge, and a sharp correction after 2022.

Act 3 · Which roles are in demand

Software dominates web development collapses

Not all tech roles tell the same story. Software programmers remain the most advertised occupation by far, while demand for web developers has collapsed by over 80% since 2015 likely displaced by AI tools. Click any occupation in the legend to highlight it.

Act 4 · Winners and losers

Who got hit hardest after the 2022 peak?

Every ICT occupation lost ground after the 2022 hiring boom. But the scale of decline varies dramatically - software programmers shed thousands of vacancies while web developers effectively disappeared from job boards entirely.

Act 5 · The talent gap

Can't fill it locally importing it from abroad

As tech job vacancies persisted despite falling employment, Australia increasingly turned to overseas skilled workers. Meanwhile the business ecosystem keeps growing but most tech companies are solo operators, not large employers.
